SalesTeam Advisors

Generic, cross-lead strategy — for advice about one specific lead, use that lead's page in the Dashboard instead. Company info, API key, message templates, and other general settings live on the separate Settings page.

Sales Mentor

A senior sales expert on call 24/7 — ask strategic questions ("Which lead should I prioritize this week?", "How could I improve my lead-gen process?"). It looks up real lead data via tool calls only when a question actually needs it, and can draft a message for you as part of its answer.

Customer Voice

Bounce a message or approach off a realistic buyer before you send it. Name a specific lead ("How would Gabel react to this: ...") and it grounds itself in their real post; ask a general question ("What's your main pain point with AI projects today?") and it answers as a typical buyer in this space.
