Setup

The 12 Setup steps: who you are, which companies and people to look for (location, size, industry, target contacts), what you sell and can offer, companies to include or exclude, and how big your list should be. Message / Response Language

      
      
      

        This is the DEFAULT language for everything SalesTeam's AI writes - the Sales Mentor's chat replies,
        Customer Voice's replies, drafted LinkedIn messages, and every other AI-generated text in the
        extension. The one exception: when Customer Voice is grounded in one specific real lead (post) - via
        get_lead_details - it replies in THAT lead's own post language instead, whatever it is - a French
        lead gets a French reaction even if this is set to English, since that's what a real reply from them
        would look like. Everywhere else, this setting is what's actually used.

Team

      
      

        

          Work as a team: colleagues share one set of accounts, contacts and leads (posts) through a OneDrive folder your
          team owns. Every member's SalesTeam reads and writes that folder - there is no SalesTeam server and no sign-in.
          Your API key, User Profile, drafts, scanner settings and LinkedIn limits stay personal.

Create a team

        
You become the Team Lead. Your accounts, contacts, leads (posts), Setup and rules become the team's
          starting data. Everyone in the team sees the shared data, including who contacted whom.

        
    
          
  1. First, below: click Choose your OneDrive folder…. In the window that opens, click OneDrive in the
            list on the left (with a work account it is called "OneDrive - your company") and click Select Folder.
            If Chrome asks whether SalesTeam may view and edit the files, choose Allow on every visit. SalesTeam creates
            the team's folder in it.
    2. 
          
  2. Enter your name, the team's name, and the colleagues you invite (name and e-mail). You can invite more
            later (Settings > Team > Add member…).
    3. 
          
  3. Click Create the team…. SalesTeam creates the folder SalesTeam - team name in your OneDrive
            folder, saves a full backup of your data and copies it into the folder - the messages below the button show how
            far it is. This can take a minute.
    4. 
          
  4. Then open File Explorer > OneDrive, right-click the new folder and choose Always keep on this device
            (on Windows 11 you may first need Show more options).
    5. 
          
  5. Right-click the folder again, choose Share, type your colleagues' e-mail addresses, check that they
            can edit, and click Send. SalesTeam cannot share it for you.
    6. 
          
  6. Each colleague then opens Settings > Team > Join a team… and accepts your invitation.
    7.

Join a team

        
Click Find my team…, choose your team and your invitation, and click Join as ….
          The first time, Chrome asks for your OneDrive folder: click OneDrive on the left
          ("OneDrive - your company" with a work account), then Select Folder and Allow on every visit.

        

          Joining replaces the accounts, contacts, leads (posts), Setup and rules in this browser with the team's.
          Accounts only you have are listed for you to tick: the ones you tick go into the team, assigned to you. A full
          backup is saved first. Your API key, User Profile, drafts and Activity Log stay as they are.
        

        

          Your team is not in the list? How to get it there
          
    
            
  1. Open the e-mail in which your Team Lead shared the team folder and click Open. The folder opens in
              OneDrive in your browser. (No e-mail? Open OneDrive in the browser and click Shared on the left.)
    2. 
            
  2. Click Add shortcut to My files (at the top, or under … next to the folder's name).
    3. 
            
  3. Open File Explorer > OneDrive. After a minute the folder appears there. Right-click it and choose
              Always keep on this device, then wait until its files show a green tick.
    4. 
            
  4. Back here, click Look again (or Find my team…).
    5.

Restore

      

        Bring your data back from a SalesTeam backup file - the backup zip made on the Backup page (or an older
        .json backup). This is what to use after reinstalling SalesTeam or when setting it up on another computer.
      

      

        After you pick the file you choose what to restore: settings, accounts / contacts / initiatives,
        your edits to them, leads (posts) and conversations, the activity log, and display preferences. Only the parts you
        tick are replaced; everything else stays as it is. A safety copy of your current data is saved first.
      

      
      
      

      

        Backups are kept in the folder chosen on the Backup page.
        
      

    


    

      
Backup

      

        One backup holds everything - settings, accounts, contacts, leads (posts), chats and the activity log - as a single dated zip.
        The zip also contains your accounts and leads as CSV spreadsheets, ready to open in Excel or upload to another system.
      


      
      

      

        
        
        
Chrome only tells SalesTeam the folder's name, never its full path. If you want the path shown here, paste it once - it is for display only and is cleared if you pick a different folder.

      

      
      

      
      
      
      

        SalesTeam makes the daily backup the first time it is open at or after this time - it cannot run while Chrome
        or the extension is closed. Daily backups are saved in a folder per month.
