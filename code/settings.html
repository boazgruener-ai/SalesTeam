What We Offer

Ideal Customer Profile

Who you're specifically targeting — company size, geography, industry, what they're investing in. Separate from "What We Offer" on purpose: this is about the customer, not the product. Shared by the same AI features, so they can judge fit against your actual target profile, not just topical overlap.

Location Filter

Restrict leads to your actual sales territory, using each lead's own location (filled in from a Job listing's own location, or from an opt-in profile visit for Posts - see the Dashboard's "Extract Companies from Profiles"). A lead whose location is confidently classified outside what you choose below gets marked Irrelevant automatically - reviewable and reversible, exactly like a Negative Topic below, never a silent delete. A lead with no location data yet, or text that can't be confidently classified, is never touched. This makes a Topic's own location keywords unnecessary going forward (existing topics are untouched either way).

Prioritization Rules

These layer on top of the Sales Mentor's own judgment for a lead whose company qualifies as a Target Account (above). A Decisive rule fixes the priority outright - the Sales Mentor never sees that lead. A Ceiling/Floor rule instead caps or raises whatever the Mentor itself would have said. The Sales Mentor is always the base decision and can't be disabled - disable a rule here to let it decide those leads entirely on its own instead.
