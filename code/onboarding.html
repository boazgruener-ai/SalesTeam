Who should we look for at each company?

Exact job titles, one per line, and/or broader title keywords, one per line. At least one of the two is required - this is who a future contact-discovery scan searches each company for.

Any competitors to exclude? (optional)

One LinkedIn company-page URL per line - not a company name, since names are too easy to mismatch (legal suffixes, short vs. long names, misspellings). Each line is checked as you go.

Any recruiters or staffing agencies to exclude? (optional)

Same idea as competitors, and for the same reason - one LinkedIn company-page URL per line, not a name. Pre-filled with well-known global agencies to save you the lookup; add your local ones and remove any that don't apply, or clear the list entirely if you'd rather skip this.
