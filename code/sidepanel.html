SalesTeam

Settings (Topics, filters, personas, etc.) and Leads (including Sales Mentor / Customer Voice chat history) are backed up as two separate files on purpose - restoring one never touches or rolls back the other. Both are downloaded automatically before every scan, in addition to whenever you export manually here.

Posts Search

Topics

Want more Posts like your best ones? "Suggest Lookalike Topics" looks at your highest-priority (P1-P3) Post leads and suggests new keywords based on what made them strong matches — nothing is added until you review and accept each one. Job listings aren't used here (no post text to learn from).

Job Search (Jobs vertical)

A geoId is a LinkedIn-internal location code, not free text. To find one: on linkedin.com/jobs, pick that location from the Location filter, then copy the geoId= value from the address bar.

Negative Topics (Lead Filters)

Same idea as a Topic above, just inverted: any lead matching one is noise, not a prospect - checked after search results come back, never sent to LinkedIn. A match auto-sets the lead's status to "Irrelevant" (reviewable/reversible any time on the Dashboard, never deleted - distinct from "Dismissed", which is always a person's own decision). The two below are built in (can't be removed, keywords fully editable) - add your own for any other kind of noise you keep seeing.

If you want to apply the negative filters above to existing leads right now, without running a new scan, click "Apply Negative Filters" below. It may change some leads' status from "Irrelevant" to "New," or from "New" to "Irrelevant," depending on what your filters currently match.

Search Quality

Not finding enough good Post leads? "Analyze Post Search Quality" has the Sales Mentor look at your current Topics, Negative Topics, and your unactioned Post leads - including ones marked Irrelevant, so it can catch an over-aggressive Negative Topic keyword killing good leads, not just suggest narrowing further. Diagnoses what's likely limiting quality and suggests specific keyword changes to try - nothing is changed until you review and accept each one.
