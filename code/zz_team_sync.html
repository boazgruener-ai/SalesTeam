1. The team folder

    
Use a NEW, EMPTY folder in OneDrive for creating the team (not the step 0 test folder). Joining: pick the same folder in the other profile.

     
    

  


  

    
2a. Create a team (first profile)

    
Your accounts, contacts, leads and shared settings become the team's starting data. A full backup is taken first.

     
    
    

  


  

    
2b. Join the team (second profile)

    
Joining replaces this profile's accounts, contacts, leads and shared settings with the team's. Your API key, drafts, scanner settings and other personal data stay. A full backup is taken first. Stop the pipeline before joining.

    

     
    

  


  

    
3. Leave

    
Stops syncing. Your data stays as it is now (a solo copy); your files stay in the team folder.
