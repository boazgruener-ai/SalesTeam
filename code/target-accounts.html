Find & Merge Duplicates

The same company sometimes appears twice - for example once from your research file and once from LinkedIn Discovery. Each proposal below lists accounts that look like the same company (same name or same LinkedIn page). Pick the one to keep and press Merge - its contacts, initiatives, sources and Post leads move over. If they are really different companies, press Keep separate and they will not be proposed again.

Merge duplicate accounts

Combine two entries for the same company into one. The other account's contacts, initiatives, sources and Post leads move to the account you keep, and its name is kept as an alternative name.

    Export to HubSpot

    Saves two files you can import into HubSpot (Contacts → Import): one for companies and one for contacts. Import the companies file first, then the contacts file - HubSpot links each contact to its company by name. The columns use HubSpot's own names, so HubSpot maps most of them for you; the "SalesTeam ..." columns (priority, score, status) can be created as new HubSpot properties in the import's mapping step.

    Note: HubSpot's Industry field only accepts its own list of values; if your industry text is not recognized, skip that column in the mapping step.

    Import from HubSpot

    In HubSpot, export your companies and/or your contacts as a CSV file (Contacts or Companies → Export), then choose the file here - one file at a time; do the companies first. SalesTeam only adds what it does not have yet: companies are matched by name, contacts by name and company. Nothing you already have is changed or removed.

    Import Research Workbook

    Load the Excel workbook (.xlsx) that the SalesTeam research prompt produces - in ChatGPT or any other AI assistant - or one you filled in yourself from the template. Importing replaces the accounts, contacts and initiatives currently loaded. (To bring back a SalesTeam backup, use "Restore Accounts & Contacts from Backup…" instead.)

    Restore Accounts & Contacts from Backup

    Bring back accounts, contacts and initiatives from a SalesTeam backup - the backup zip made by Backup & Restore, or an older .json file. You choose what to restore; the parts you tick replace what is loaded now, and a safety copy of your current data is saved first. (To load a research workbook, use "Import Research Workbook…" instead.)

    Resolve LinkedIn Company IDs

    Experimental: look up each Target Account company by name on LinkedIn to resolve its numeric company ID - needed for a future feature that scopes Post search to only these companies' employees.

    Discover Contacts for Existing Companies

    Look up contacts at each existing Target Account company via its own LinkedIn People tab, using the Setup wizard's target-contact profile - only visits a company currently below the contact-count threshold.

    Fetch Company Size

    Visit each company (imported or discovered) currently missing a real employee count on its own LinkedIn page and read it off there - LinkedIn's company-search result cards never show size, only a company's own page does.

    Prioritize Companies

    Score every eligible company's Priority (P1-P5): imported research is trusted directly when confident, otherwise a free deterministic pre-score (Location/Size/Industry/matched Contacts) runs, optionally refined by an AI Strategic Fit judgment.
