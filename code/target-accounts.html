Add Discovery results to your Target Accounts?

This adds the companies/contacts below to your Target Accounts list - click a company to check it on LinkedIn first if you want. A company already on your list won't be duplicated; it's just left as is.

Anything added can be removed again afterward from its own Account/Contact page.

Run pipeline now

Works through your accounts one at a time, best first: re-checks each LinkedIn company page (reading the employee count on the same visit), looks up the LinkedIn profile of contacts you already know by name, and finds contacts where none is known. Each account is then re-scored.

It opens a small separate LinkedIn window that stays in the background - you can keep working. It stops by itself at 75 LinkedIn page visits in any 24 hours. Every value is saved the moment it is found.

Advanced tools → Research accounts on the web

Fills in what each account still lacks - HQ, employees, industry, initiatives, senior people and a short summary - from the company's own pages and web searches. People found are added as contacts; no LinkedIn page is visited.

Listed companies go first, then by priority. It costs money on your Anthropic API key; the report at the end shows the cost per account.

          Research accounts on the web

          Searches the public web for each chosen account (website, registers, reports, news) with your own Anthropic API key and saves a briefing with sources on the account, adds the initiatives it finds to the Initiatives list, and fills in empty fields. It runs in the background: you can keep using this page while it works. No LinkedIn activity is used.

          Advanced tools → Resolve LinkedIn Company IDs

          Looks each account up on LinkedIn to find its company ID - the Scanner can only search companies that have one.

          Advanced tools → Discover Contacts for Existing Companies

          Finds contacts on each account's LinkedIn People tab that match your target-contact profile. Only accounts below the contact threshold are visited.

          Advanced tools → Fetch Company Size

          Reads the employee count from the company's own LinkedIn page, for each account that has none yet.

          Advanced tools → Prioritize Companies

          Scores each account's priority (P1-P5) from location, size, industry and contacts; research you imported is used where it is confident. AI Strategic Fit can refine the score.
