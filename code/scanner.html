Posts Search Configuration

Topics

Want more Posts like your best ones? "Suggest Lookalike Topics" looks at your highest-priority (P1-P3) Post leads and suggests new keywords based on what made them strong matches — nothing is added until you review and accept each one. Job listings aren't used here (no post text to learn from).

Job Listing Search

A geoId is a LinkedIn-internal location code, not free text. To find one: on linkedin.com/jobs, pick that location from the Location filter, then copy the geoId= value from the address bar.

Negative Topics (Lead Filters)

Same idea as a Topic above, just inverted: any lead matching a negative topic is noise, not a prospect. Negative topics are checked after the search results come back and are never sent to LinkedIn. The company filters at the top (competitors, recruiting companies, existing customers and partners) are one checkbox each, because their company lists are kept in the Setup wizard. Add your own custom filter for any other kind of noise you keep seeing.

To apply the negative filters above to your existing leads right now, without running a new scan, click "Apply Negative Filters" below. Existing leads with status New that match a filter are removed from your lists (nothing is deleted); leads that no longer match any filter come back.

Search Quality

Not finding enough good Post leads? "Analyze Post Search Quality" has the Sales Mentor look at your current Topics, Negative Topics, and your unactioned Post leads - including ones marked Irrelevant, so it can catch an over-aggressive Negative Topic keyword killing good leads, not just suggest narrowing further. Diagnoses what's likely limiting quality and suggests specific keyword changes to try - nothing is changed until you review and accept each one.
